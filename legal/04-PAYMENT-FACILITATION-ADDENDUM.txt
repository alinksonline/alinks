================================================================================

1. ROLE OF PARTIES
  (a) Tenant is the SELLER OF RECORD for all goods and services sold on mini-site
  (b) Artix provides payment facilitation technology connecting Tenant to licensed
      payment partners (Razorpay, PhonePe Payment Gateway)
  (c) Artix is NOT a bank, payment aggregator (unless separately licensed), or
      seller of Tenant's products
  (d) Settlement of customer payments goes to Tenant's designated bank account
      via partner settlement APIs (Razorpay Route / PhonePe partner rules)

2. TENANT OBLIGATIONS
  (a) Provide accurate KYC: PAN, bank account, business proof, GSTIN if registered
  (b) Comply with RBI, PG, and NPCI rules applicable to merchants
  (c) Handle refunds, chargebacks, and customer payment disputes
  (d) Not use checkout for illegal goods, money laundering, or prohibited categories
  (e) Display accurate prices, MRP, taxes as applicable
  (f) Maintain records of sales for GST and income tax

3. ARTIX OBLIGATIONS
  (a) Provide checkout UI integrating partner PGs
  (b) Submit Tenant KYC to partners for sub-merchant / linked account onboarding
  (c) Route webhooks to record orders in Tenant's Google Sheet / Supabase
  (d) Do not store card numbers; use hosted checkout / tokenization only

4. FEES
  (a) Payment gateway fees (~2% + GST) deducted by partner from settlement
  (b) ALINKS subscription fee is separate (SaaS billing to Artix)
  (c) Artix platform transaction fee: [0% at launch — LAWYER/CA TO CONFIRM IF ADDED]
  (d) Artix may earn partner referral commission from PGs (does not increase Tenant PG fee)

5. COD (CASH ON DELIVERY)
  (a) Tenant may enable COD; no PG involved for COD collection
  (b) COD risk (fake orders, non-payment, cash handling) is 100% Tenant liability
  (c) Tenant must publish COD terms in their customer T&C

6. CHARGEBACKS & REFUNDS
  Tenant is responsible for resolving chargebacks. Artix may suspend checkout on
  excessive chargeback rates. Refund policy is Tenant's — see Tenant T&C templates.

7. DATA
  Minimal payment metadata (payment_id, amount, status) may be cached ephemerally
  for order confirmation. Full payment records: partner dashboard + Tenant Sheet.
  DPDP: purpose limitation for KYC data collected for onboarding.

8. SUSPENSION
Artix or partners may suspend payment capability for fraud, KYC failure, or breach.

9. TAX (CA TO CONFIRM)

  - TCS under GST (marketplace): [IF APPLICABLE — CA]
  - Section 194-O TDS (e-commerce operator): [IF ARTIX IS ECO — CA + LAWYER]
  - GST on Artix SaaS subscription: Artix invoices Tenant separately
