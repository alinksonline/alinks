================================================================================
PART I — TENANT BYO GATEWAY (CURRENT DEFAULT FOR SHOP CHECKOUT)
================================================================================

1. ROLE OF PARTIES
  (a) Tenant is the SELLER OF RECORD for all goods and services sold on the mini-site
  (b) Tenant is the merchant of record with their payment gateway (Razorpay etc.)
  (c) Artix provides software that creates orders and verifies payments using
      Tenant-supplied API credentials; Artix is NOT a bank, PA (unless separately
      licensed), or co-seller of Tenant products
  (d) Settlement, refunds, chargebacks, and KYC for shop sales are between Tenant
      and their payment gateway — not Artix

2. TENANT OBLIGATIONS
  (a) Maintain a valid merchant account with a licensed payment gateway
  (b) Provide accurate API keys; revoke keys when disconnecting ALINKS checkout
  (c) Comply with RBI, PG, NPCI, and tax rules applicable to merchants
  (d) Handle refunds, chargebacks, and customer payment disputes
  (e) Not use checkout for illegal goods, money laundering, or prohibited categories
  (f) Display accurate prices, MRP, taxes as applicable
  (g) Maintain sales records for GST and income tax

3. ARTIX OBLIGATIONS
  (a) Provide checkout UI and server-side order/verify using Tenant credentials
  (b) Encrypt Tenant gateway secrets at rest; never expose secrets to the browser
  (c) Write order/payment refs to Tenant storage (Sheets / BYO Supabase) per adapters
  (d) Do not store card numbers; use hosted gateway checkout / tokenization only

4. FEES
  (a) Payment gateway fees: charged by Tenant's PG under Tenant's merchant contract
  (b) ALINKS subscription / module fees: separate SaaS billing to Artix (Part II)
  (c) Artix does not deduct GMV or take a share of shop settlement under BYO model

5. COD (CASH ON DELIVERY)
  (a) Tenant may enable COD; no PG involved for COD collection
  (b) COD risk (fake orders, non-payment, cash handling) is 100% Tenant liability
  (c) Tenant must publish COD terms in their customer T&C (Q020)

6. CHARGEBACKS & REFUNDS
  Tenant resolves chargebacks with their PG. Artix may suspend platform checkout
  features for abuse. Refund policy is Tenant's — see Tenant T&C templates.

7. DATA
  Minimal payment metadata (payment_id, amount, status) may be stored as platform
  checkout session refs. Full payment records: Tenant PG dashboard + Tenant Sheet.
  DPDP: Tenant is fiduciary for end-customer order data in their storage.

8. ACKNOWLEDGMENT (BYO)
  By connecting API keys and enabling online checkout, Tenant confirms:
  (i) keys belong to their merchant account; (ii) customer money settles to them;
  (iii) Artix is not facilitating settlement of shop GMV.
