1. PURPOSE
Protect the Platform, users, and public from illegal, harmful, or abusive use.

2. PROHIBITED CONTENT & CONDUCT
Tenants must NOT use ALINKS to:
  (a) Sell illegal goods or services under Indian law
  (b) Sell prescription-only medicines without valid drug licence (pharmacy vertical)
  (c) Offer medical services without valid registration (NMC/state council/AYUSH)
  (d) Impersonate doctors, hospitals, or government bodies
  (e) Sell counterfeit, stolen, or IP-infringing products
  (f) Publish false claims (health cures, guaranteed income, fake certifications)
  (g) Facilitate fraud, phishing, or payment scams
  (h) Harass, discriminate, or publish hate content
  (i) Distribute malware or attempt to breach Platform security
  (j) Scrape or overload Platform APIs abusively
  (k) Resell ALINKS access without authorization
  (l) Use affiliate links without ASCI-compliant disclosure (#ad / sponsored)

3. HIGH-RISK CATEGORIES (EXTRA SCRUTINY)
  - Pharmacy: OTC only; drug licence mandatory on file
  - Clinic/doctor: NMC/reg number mandatory; ALINKS Verify pipeline
  - Food/grocery: no false organic/FSSAI claims
  - Financial products: prohibited unless licensed

4. MODERATION
Artix may:
  - Remove or hide listings
  - Suspend checkout or publishing
  - Terminate accounts
  - Report to authorities where legally required
  - Cooperate with law enforcement and grievance requests per IT Act

5. TENANT COOPERATION
Tenant must respond to takedown notices and provide licence proof when requested.

6. REPORTING
Report abuse: abuse@alinks.online | Grievance Officer: [Doc 18]

7. NO DUTY TO MONITOR
Artix is not obligated to pre-screen all content but may do so. Intermediary
safe harbour subject to due diligence compliance. [LAWYER TO ALIGN WITH IT ACT]
