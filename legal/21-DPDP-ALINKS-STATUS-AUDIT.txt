================================================================================
WHAT IS DPDP (QUICK REFERENCE)
================================================================================
India's law governing collection, use, storage, sharing, and erasure of
digital personal data. Core obligations for ALINKS:

  1. Clear notice before processing
  2. Valid consent (or other lawful basis) for personal data
  3. Purpose limitation — collect only what is needed
  4. Security safeguards
  5. Breach notification where required
  6. Data principal rights: access, correction, erasure, grievance
  7. Children's data — additional protections
  8. Accountability via grievance officer and records

================================================================================
ALINKS DATA ROLES (DPDP-relevant)
================================================================================

TENANT DATA (business owner signs up for ALINKS)
  Examples: name, email, phone, business name, vertical, billing, IP, session
  Data fiduciary: Artix (for platform account data)
  Storage: Platform Postgres (DATABASE_URL — Neon or Supabase Postgres)
  Policy: Platform Privacy Policy (/privacy)

END-CUSTOMER DATA (salon client, kirana buyer, clinic patient)
  Examples: customer name, phone, order, booking, patient notes
  Data fiduciary: TENANT (not Artix) — tenant publishes own Privacy Policy
  Storage: Tenant Google Sheets OR tenant BYO Supabase — NOT Artix Postgres
  Artix role: Processor / technical facilitator (transient memory only)

This split is documented in:
  - ALINKS_BASELINE.txt (data rule locked)
  - src/ARCHITECTURE.txt
  - platform-documents.ts Privacy §2–3
  - legal/06-DATA-STORAGE-ADDENDUM-GOOGLE-SUPABASE.txt

================================================================================
TENANT OBLIGATIONS (remind tenants — not Artix DPDP scope for customer PII)
================================================================================

Each ALINKS tenant who collects customer data must:
  - Publish Tenant Privacy Policy on their mini-site (template: legal/07)
  - Obtain consent from their customers where required
  - Honor deletion requests for data IN THEIR Sheet/Supabase
  - Not use ALINKS for unlawful processing

Artix provides templates and storage routing; tenant remains fiduciary to
end-customers.
