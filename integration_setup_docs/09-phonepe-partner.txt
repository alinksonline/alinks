================================================================================
09 — PHONEPE PAYMENT GATEWAY (FULL APPLICATION + INTEGRATION GUIDE)
================================================================================

GOAL
  Optional second payment gateway alongside Razorpay.
  PhonePe PG surfaces UPI (including PhonePe app and Google Pay via UPI intent).

WHEN YOU NEED PHONEPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  REQUIRED?  No — Razorpay alone covers UPI, cards, GPay, PhonePe app.

  APPLY IF:
    - Razorpay partner approval is delayed
    - You want dual-gateway failover
    - Tenants specifically request PhonePe PG branding
    - You want extra partner commission stream (~0.20% per PhonePe docs)

  Baseline: apply BOTH Razorpay + PhonePe in Phase 0 (long onboarding).

GOOGLE PAY NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  You do NOT apply to Google Pay directly.
  GPay appears as UPI option inside PhonePe PG or Razorpay checkout.

PART 2 — PHONEPE PARTNER PROGRAM APPLICATION (STEP BY STEP)
================================================================================

SIGNUP URL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Primary:  https://www.phonepe.com/business-solutions/payment-gateway/partner-program/register
  Info:     https://business.phonepe.com/partner-program

STEP 1 — OPEN REGISTRATION PAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Visit partner registration URL above
  2. Click "Become a Partner Now" or "Get Started For Free"
  3. You land on PhonePe PG partner signup form

STEP 2 — FILL REGISTRATION FORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Typical fields (exact form may vary):

  Full name:           [Founder / authorised signatory name]
  Email:               artixforcoding@gmail.com (or company email)
  Mobile:              +91 [10-digit Indian number]
  Company name:        Artix Private Limited
  Company website:     https://alinks.online
  Business type:       SaaS / Technology Platform
  City:                [Your city — e.g. Vijayawada / Hyderabad / Bangalore]

  Partner type — choose based on form options:
    Technology Partner  ← ALINKS (embed PG in platform)
    NOT Service Partner (unless you only refer, not embed)

  Business description (copy-paste ready):
    "ALINKS is an India-first SaaS platform that helps small businesses build
     mobile mini-websites with online catalog, appointments, and checkout.
     We want to offer PhonePe Payment Gateway to our Pro-tier tenants so their
     end-customers can pay via UPI, cards, and wallets. Tenants are merchants
     of record; Artix embeds checkout and facilitates onboarding. We expect to
     onboard salons, clinics, kirana stores, and restaurants across India."

  Expected merchant volume:  honest estimate (e.g. 50–500 merchants in 12 months)
  Expected transaction volume: start conservative

STEP 3 — SUBMIT AND WAIT FOR OUTREACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  After signup:
    "A business representative will reach out to help you complete onboarding."

  What happens next:
    1. PhonePe sales/partner team emails or calls within 1–7 business days
    2. They schedule onboarding call
    3. You present ALINKS product demo (have app.alinks.online ready)
    4. They request company documents (list below)
    5. Partner agreement / terms shared
    6. After approval → partner dashboard access + referral link

  If no response in 7 days:
    - Reply to any PhonePe confirmation email
    - Contact via PhonePe business support channels
    - Mention: "Technology Partner application for SaaS platform ALINKS"

STEP 5 — PARTNER AGREEMENT & COMMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Per PhonePe partner program (public info):
    - Commission: ~0.20% on transactions by referred merchants
    - Merchants may get promotional pricing (e.g. 0% first 3 months — confirm on call)
    - Monthly automated commission payout to partner bank account

  Confirm exact terms on your partner call — do not assume rates.

STEP 6 — RECEIVE REFERRAL LINK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  After onboarding complete:
    PhonePe emails referral link to registered address.

  Use this to:
    - Manually onboard early tenants (beta)
    - Test merchant KYC flow

  Production goal: PhonePe PG API embedded in ALINKS (not manual referral).

================================================================================
PART 3 — ONBOARD TENANTS AS PHONEPE MERCHANTS
================================================================================

MANUAL PATH (BETA — BEFORE API INTEGRATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Share PhonePe partner referral link with tenant
  2. Tenant signs up on PhonePe PG
  3. Tenant completes their own KYC (PAN, bank, business proof)
  4. PhonePe activates merchant account (often ~3 days per PhonePe marketing)
  5. Tenant provides Merchant ID + Salt keys to ALINKS dashboard
     (OR ALINKS stores via partner API when implemented)

EMBEDDED PATH (PRODUCTION — ALINKS DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ALINKS dashboard → Payments → Enable → KYC wizard collects:
    - Business legal name
    - PAN
    - Bank account + IFSC
    - GSTIN (if registered)
    - Business category
    - Address
    - Authorised signatory details

  Artix submits to PhonePe partner API on tenant's behalf.
  Tenant accepts legal checkbox (legal/16-TOUCHPOINT-CHECKBOX-TEXT.txt).

  Money flow:
    Customer pays on tenant mini-site
      → PhonePe PG processes
      → Settlement to tenant bank (minus PG fee ~2% + GST)
      → Artix earns partner commission

PART 5 — PHONEPE VS RAZORPAY — DECISION GUIDE
================================================================================

  | Question                          | Answer                           |
  |-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
  | Need both?                        | Apply both; launch with Razorpay |
  | GPay without either?              | Impossible for websites          |
  | Which is faster to approve?       | Varies — apply both early        |
  | Tenant opens own account?         | No — Artix partner model only    |
  | COD?                              | No PG needed — tenant setting    |
  | SaaS subscription billing?        | Use Razorpay (doc 08 Flow A)     |
