================================================================================
08 — RAZORPAY (FULL APPLICATION + INTEGRATION GUIDE)
================================================================================

GOAL
  ALINKS needs Razorpay for TWO separate money flows:

  Flow A — SaaS billing (tenant pays Artix):
    Basic ₹599/mo, Pro ₹1599/mo, Enterprise ₹6999/mo
    Artix is merchant of record.

  Flow B — Tenant commerce (customer pays tenant via ALINKS checkout):
    UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe app, Paytm, BHIM), cards, netbanking
    Tenant is seller of record; money settles to tenant bank.
    Requires Razorpay Partner Program + Route / sub-merchant APIs.

GOOGLE PAY
  No separate Google Pay merchant API. GPay appears as a UPI option inside
  Razorpay Checkout — customer taps GPay → UPI app opens.

PART 2 — APPLY FOR RAZORPAY MERCHANT ACCOUNT (FLOW A — SAAS BILLING)
================================================================================

This is Artix's own account to collect ALINKS subscription fees from tenants.

STEP 1 — SIGN UP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Go to: https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup
     OR https://accounts.razorpay.com/auth/?intent=signup&product=payment_gateway
  2. Enter:
       Email:     artixforcoding@gmail.com (or company email)
       Mobile:    your Indian phone (+91)
       Password:  strong unique password
  3. Verify email + mobile OTP

STEP 2 — BUSINESS DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fill the onboarding form honestly:

  Business name:     Artix Private Limited  (or "Artix" if proprietorship)
  Business type:     Software / SaaS / Technology
  Category:          Software as a Service (SaaS)
  Sub-category:      Business productivity / website builder

  Business description (copy-paste ready):
    "ALINKS is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that helps Indian small businesses
     create mobile-first mini-websites with booking, catalog, and WhatsApp ordering.
     We charge monthly subscriptions (Basic, Pro, Enterprise) to business owners
     who use our dashboard at app.alinks.online."

  Website:           https://alinks.online
  Expected volume:   Start low (e.g. ₹50,000–2,00,000/month) — adjust honestly
  Average ticket:    ₹599 – ₹6,999 per transaction (subscription)

STEP 3 — SUBMIT KYC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dashboard → Account & Settings → Business Details → Complete KYC

  Upload:
    - PAN
    - Business proof (GST / MSME / incorporation cert)
    - Bank account proof (cancelled cheque)
    - Address proof
    - Signatory photo (sometimes required)

  Activation time: typically 2–7 business days (can be faster).

STEP 4 — START IN TEST MODE (IMMEDIATELY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Before live KYC completes, Test Mode works for development.

  Dashboard → toggle "Test Mode" ON (top bar)

  Settings → API Keys → Generate Test Key
    RAZORPAY_KEY_ID=rzp_test_xxxxxxxx
    RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET=xxxxxxxx

  Add to local .env and Vercel (mark Sensitive).

STEP 5 — GO LIVE (AFTER KYC APPROVED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Dashboard → toggle Test Mode OFF
  2. Settings → API Keys → Generate Live Key
     RAZORPAY_KEY_ID=rzp_live_xxxxxxxx
     RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET=xxxxxxxx
  3. Never expose KEY_SECRET in browser or GitHub.

STEP 6 — CREATE SUBSCRIPTION PLANS (SAAS TIERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dashboard → Subscriptions → Plans → Create Plan

  | Plan name   | Amount   | Billing   | ALINKS tier  |
  |-------------|----------|-----------|--------------|
  | ALINKS Basic| ₹599     | Monthly   | Basic        |
  | ALINKS Pro  | ₹1599    | Monthly   | Pro          |
  | ALINKS Ent  | ₹6999    | Monthly   | Enterprise   |

  Optional annual plans (baseline pricing):
    Basic annual equiv: ₹499/mo billed yearly
    Pro annual equiv:   ₹1499/mo billed yearly

  Product rule: 14-day Pro trial — no card at signup; charge from Day 15.

STEP 7 — WEBHOOKS (SAAS BILLING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dashboard → Settings → Webhooks → Add New Webhook

  URL: https://alinks.online/api/webhooks/razorpay

  Events to enable:
    payment.captured
    subscription.activated
    subscription.charged
    subscription.cancelled
    subscription.halted
    invoice.paid

  Copy Webhook Secret → Vercel env:
    RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_xxxx

  Code: src/platform/payments/razorpay.ts → verifyRazorpaySignature()

PART 3 — APPLY FOR RAZORPAY PARTNER PROGRAM (FLOW B — TENANT CHECKOUT)
================================================================================

This is what lets ALINKS onboard tenants as sub-merchants so THEIR customers
can pay via Razorpay checkout on tenant mini-sites. Artix earns partner commission.

WHICH PARTNER TYPE TO CHOOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ALINKS = Technology Partner (SaaS provider)

  NOT Service Partner (agencies/freelancers who manually refer clients).

  Technology Partner = embed payments inside your platform via APIs.

SIGNUP URL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  https://accounts.razorpay.com/auth/?redirecturl=https%3A%2F%2Fdashboard.razorpay.com%2Fapp%2Fpartners&auth_intent=signup&user_type=partner&x-country-code=IN

  OR: https://razorpay.com/partners/ → "Become a Partner" → Sign Up

STEP 1 — PARTNER REGISTRATION FORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Use SAME email as merchant account if possible (easier linking).

  Fields you will fill:

  Company name:        Artix Private Limited
  Partner type:        Technology Partner
  Business category:   SaaS / E-commerce enablement
  Website:             https://alinks.online
  Number of clients:   Start with estimate (e.g. 10–100 in year 1)

  Description (copy-paste ready):
    "ALINKS is a SaaS website builder for Indian SMBs (salons, clinics, kirana,
     restaurants). We want to embed Razorpay Payment Gateway so our Pro tenants
     can accept UPI, cards, and wallets on their mini-sites without opening their
     own Razorpay account. Tenants are sellers of record; we facilitate onboarding
     and checkout via our platform. We need Partner APIs for sub-merchant onboarding
     and Razorpay Route for settlement to tenant bank accounts."

STEP 2 — PARTNER KYC (SEPARATE FROM MERCHANT KYC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Partner dashboard may request:
    - Company incorporation documents
    - Director PAN
    - GST certificate
    - Brief on your integration plan
    - Live product demo or staging URL

  Timeline: 1–4 weeks for partner approval (varies).

STEP 3 — REQUEST ONBOARDING APIs (CRITICAL FOR ALINKS UX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  For tenants to complete KYC INSIDE ALINKS dashboard (not on razorpay.com):

  1. Go to: https://razorpay.com/partners/onboarding-apis/
  2. Click "Talk to sales" / "Contact Sales"
  3. Fill form:
       Name, phone, company: Artix / ALINKS
       Message: "SaaS platform embedding PG onboarding for SMB tenants"
  4. Razorpay sales team schedules call
  5. After approval, you get access to Onboarding API docs:
       https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/onboarding-api/

  Without Onboarding APIs: tenants must sign up on Razorpay separately (worse UX).
  With Onboarding APIs: KYC happens inside ALINKS → much better conversion.

STEP 4 — RAZORPAY ROUTE (TENANT SETTLEMENT SPLITS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Route lets customer payment settle directly to tenant bank (minus PG fee).

  Request Route activation via partner account manager or:
    Dashboard → Partner → discuss "Route" / "Linked Accounts"

  Flow:
    Customer pays ₹500 on tenant kirana site
      → Razorpay captures payment
      → Route transfers to tenant linked account / bank
      → Artix partner commission credited separately

  Docs: https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/

STEP 5 — ONBOARD TENANTS AS SUB-MERCHANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Option A — Manual (early beta):
    Partner Dashboard → Affiliate Accounts → + Add New Clients
    Enter tenant email → Send Invite → Assist with KYC

  Option B — API (production):
    Onboarding API creates sub-merchant from ALINKS dashboard KYC form

  Option C — Referral link (lowest effort):
    Partner Dashboard → Share Referral Link → send to tenant

  Tenant KYC fields ALINKS collects (dashboard wizard):
    - Business legal name
    - PAN
    - Bank account + IFSC
    - GSTIN (optional)
    - Business address
    - Category (retail, services, healthcare, etc.)
    - Aadhaar (if Razorpay requires)

  Tenant must accept checkbox (legal/16-TOUCHPOINT-CHECKBOX-TEXT.txt):
    "I authorise ALINKS to submit my KYC to Razorpay/PhonePe for onboarding"

  Activation: typically 24–72 hours after KYC submitted.

STEP 6 — PARTNER COMMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Artix earns fixed % on tenant transaction volume (paid by Razorpay, not tenant).
  View earnings: Partner Dashboard → Commissions

  Service partner referral link model: ~0.1% on affiliate transactions.
  Technology partner terms: negotiated on partner call.

  RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_xxx

TENANT CHECKOUT FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Customer adds to cart on tenant public site
  2. ALINKS server: createRazorpayOrder() — src/platform/payments/razorpay.ts
  3. Razorpay Checkout.js opens (hosted — never touch card numbers on Artix servers)
  4. Customer pays via UPI / card / GPay / PhonePe app
  5. Webhook payment.captured → write order to tenant Google Sheet
  6. Show confirmation to customer

TEST PAYMENT (TEST MODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Test card:  4111 1111 1111 1111, any future expiry, any CVV
  Test UPI:   success@razorpay (see Razorpay test docs)
  Verify webhook received in dashboard → Webhooks → Logs

COD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cash on Delivery = tenant toggle only. No Razorpay involved.
  Tenant bears all COD risk (legal/04-PAYMENT-FACILITATION-ADDENDUM.txt).
