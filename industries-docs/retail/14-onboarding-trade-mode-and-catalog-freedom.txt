1. ONBOARDING — TRADE MODE (FROZEN LABELS)
================================================================================

  Question (registration / business setup):
    “How do you sell?”

  Options (exactly these three — frozen):
    ○ Retail only          — I sell to end customers
    ○ Wholesale only       — I sell bulk / to other businesses
    ○ Retail + Wholesale   — Both (hybrid)

  Internal values:
    retail_only | wholesale_only | hybrid

  Effects:
    retail_only     → storefront B2C on; wholesale path off
    wholesale_only  → wholesale path on; consumer grid de-emphasized
    hybrid          → both paths; dual prices allowed on products

  Can change later in Settings (with confirmation).

================================================================================
2. CATALOG FREEDOM — SELL ANYTHING
================================================================================

  LOCKED: Product categories are OPEN TAXONOMY. Tenant can sell any legal
  product line they name — not a closed pick-list of “only fashion” or “only
  kitchen”.

  Examples (non-exhaustive — if they can stock it, they can list it):
    Electronics, electricals, plumbing, gadgets, mobiles, laptops, accessories,
    toys, games, kitchenware, home, furniture (as product, not RE),
    plants, seeds, nursery,
    herbal, Ayurvedic products (OTC retail — not clinical diagnosis),
    veggies / produce / grocery packs,
    fashion, clothes, footwear, beauty,
    hardware, tools, paints, automotive accessories,
    books, stationery, sports, pet supplies, …
    “Whatever they name” via custom categories

  Superadmin may SEED category packs (Fashion, Kirana, Electronics, …) for
  faster setup — seeds are optional templates, never a hard limit on what
  they can add.

  Legal / AUP:
    Prohibited items still blocked by platform AUP (weapons, illegal drugs, etc.)
    — enforcement policy, not category taxonomy limit.

  NOT for this industry:
    Restaurant cooked meals / dine-in → food_industry
    Land/flats as title sale → real_estate
    Doctor consult slots → bookings_industry
