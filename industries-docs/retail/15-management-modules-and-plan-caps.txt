15 — RETAIL MANAGEMENT MODULES + SMART PLAN CAPS (ANTI-CHAOS / ANTI-LOSS)
================================================================================
Folder: industries-docs/retail/
Goal: Streamline modules and caps so ALINKS does not go chaotic across
      industries and does not give away unlimited cost (storage, support, PG
      support load) for free.

Cross-industry consistency:
  Same pattern as food / bookings / RE:
    Layer 1 Website plan + Layer 2 paid ops modules + FREE Google where applicable
    Yearly = ×12 − 5–10% → charm …99
    Multi-outlet 5–10% off extra branches
    Seasonal coupons on SOFTWARE fees only (not a free-for-all forever)

================================================================================
1. MANAGEMENT MODULE LIST (RETAIL)
================================================================================

  Keep the list SHORT and name-stable. Price/upscale against this menu only.

  --- CORE CATALOGUE (always related to “store”) ---
  ID                    Label (UI)              What it does
  --------------------  ----------------------  --------------------------------
  rt.inventory          Inventory               Products, SKUs, stock, media
  rt.categories         Categories              Open category tree + filters
  rt.variations         Variations              Size/color/pack/unit option sets
  rt.multi_brand        Multi-brand             Brands, logos, brand filters
  rt.storefront         Storefront              Public B2C shop grid/PDP

  --- TRADE MODE ---
  rt.wholesale          Wholesale               MOQ, tier prices, B2B path/enquiry

  --- ORDERS & MONEY (tenant PG — Artix does not take GMV) ---
  rt.checkout           Checkout                Cart + Razorpay BYO + COD
  rt.orders             Orders desk             Order list, status, export
                                                (may ship inside checkout/pro)

  --- FULFILMENT ---
  rt.delivery           Delivery                Address orders, fee, optional
                                                riders/third-party (pattern
                                                from food delivery — simplified)
  rt.pickup             Store pickup            Collect at shop/stall

  --- GROWTH / OPS ---
  rt.staff              Staff (dashboard)       Extra logins for catalogue/orders
                                                online — NOT in-store POS cashiers
  rt.reports            Reports (lite)          Sales summary (later; paid)

  --- NOT A MODULE (and usually NOT NEEDED) ---
  rt.multilocation / multi-outlet
    REMOVED from retail core module list (LOCKED product decision).
    Why: ALINKS is NOT in-store billing / multi-till POS software.
    One online shop + one catalogue serves the brand. Physical shops do not
    each need a separate “outlet seat” for website commerce.
    Optional later (only if demanded): simple list of “pickup addresses”
    as plain data fields — NOT per-shop inventory, NOT 5–10% multi-store SKU.
    Food multi-branch / bookings multi-clinic stay in THOSE industries
    (kitchens, clinics have real per-location ops). Do not copy that tax
    into retail by default.

  --- NOT SEPARATE PAID MODULES (included / platform) ---
  google.calendar       FREE if they book demos/visits
  WhatsApp CTA          Included in website plan
  Open category naming  Included with inventory

  AVOID MODULE EXPLOSION
    Do NOT create paid SKUs per category (no “Electronics module”,
    “Ayurveda module”). Category = data. Module = capability.
    Do NOT invent multi-store POS modules we are not building.

================================================================================
2. WHAT “DELIVERY” + “INVENTORY” MEAN HERE
================================================================================

  Inventory (rt.inventory)
    Heart of retail: product CRUD, stock, live/hide, media WebP.
    Caps on product COUNT are the main cost-control lever.

  Delivery (rt.delivery)
    Shop sends products to customer address (not restaurant kitchen board).
    Optional: own rider list lite OR third-party note (same spirit as food 04,
    lighter v1).
    Pickup is separate small module or free with delivery.

================================================================================
3. BUNDLE SUGGESTIONS (UPSELL PATH — NOT CHAOS)
================================================================================

  Starter (Website)
    Catalogue display + WA + low product cap
    trade mode chosen at onboarding (retail / wholesale / both) but
    wholesale PATH may be teaser until rt.wholesale purchased

  Shop Pro
    Inventory full + storefront + categories + variations + checkout
    Cap mid (e.g. 200 products)

  Wholesale Pro
    Inventory + wholesale + bulk enquiry/cart + higher cap

  Market Complete
    Shop Pro + wholesale + multi_brand + delivery

  À LA CARTE: every module has its own price; tenant picks any subset.
  Bundles are optional shortcuts only (see 00-MODULE-ALACARTE-PRICING.txt).


================================================================================
4. PRODUCT CAPS — SMART STREAMLINE (LOCKED DIRECTION)
================================================================================

  WHY CAPS
    Each product = media storage, support surface, search, Sheet rows.
    Unlimited free SKUs = loss + chaos.

  TIER TABLE (default numbers — Superadmin can tune; do not invent 50 tiers)

    Plan / bundle           Max products    Notes
    ----------------------  --------------  --------------------------------
    Website / Starter       25              Align Basic commerce skill
    Growth / Shop           200             Align Pro commerce skill
    Business / Market       2,000           Align Enterprise-style
    Custom                  negotiated      Only high-touch

  Cap counts ALL products (draft+live) or live-only — implement LIVE+draft
  with soft warn; hard block on create when over.

  Brands / categories
    Soft limits: e.g. 50 brands, 100 categories on Starter; raise with plan
    Do not sell “unlimited categories” as a separate SKU.

  Variations
    Soft limit option sets (e.g. 20 sets Starter / 100 Pro)
    Variant SKUs count toward product cap OR separate variant cap —
    RECOMMEND: each sellable variant SKU counts as 1 toward product cap
    (prevents 1 product × 500 variants abuse)

  Orders
    No hard cap v1; rate-limit abuse; storage on tenant Sheets/Blob

================================================================================
5. PRICING CONTROL (ANTI-LOSS)
================================================================================

  Revenue levers (use these, not infinite free features):
    1. Website plan tier (primary)
    2. Product cap tier (primary)
    3. Wholesale module
    4. Multi-brand module
    5. Delivery module
    6. Checkout included in Pro OR small add-on
    7. Seasonal coupons on SOFTWARE only (40–50% campaigns, time-boxed)
    (No multi-outlet seat tax for retail — we are not POS.)

  Do NOT:
    • Give Enterprise caps on Starter “for growth”
    • Create 30 micro-modules per product type
    • Include unlimited image storage without Blob cost plan
    • Mix Artix SaaS fee with shop GMV commission (unless future explicit product)

  Support cost
    Higher caps → higher plan only
    Wholesale + delivery → clearer paid modules (ops support)

================================================================================
6. CROSS-INDUSTRY NON-CHAOS RULES
================================================================================

  Shared patterns across Food / Bookings / RE / Retail:
    ✓ Website plan first
    ✓ Short module menu per industry
    ✓ Caps by plan
    ✓ Yearly charm …99
    ✓ Multi-location 5–10% off
    ✓ Free Google Calendar where events apply
    ✓ Tenant owns customer money rails
    ✓ Superadmin industry catalog

  Different only where business differs:
    Food = channels (dine-in/delivery/kitchen)
    Bookings = slots + compliance
    RE = no checkout + Property-Bank privacy
    Retail = products + retail/wholesale + multi-brand

  Superadmin is source of truth for which modules each industry type may buy
  — prevents random module assignment chaos.

================================================================================
7. ONBOARDING → MODULE ENTITLEMENT FLOW
================================================================================

  1. Pick website plan (sets product cap)
  2. Pick trade mode: Retail only | Wholesale only | Retail + Wholesale
  3. Create shop → add products (any categories)
  4. Upsell prompts when:
       - Hit 80% of product cap → upgrade plan
       - Adds MOQ → suggest rt.wholesale
       - Adds 2nd brand → suggest rt.multi_brand
       - Wants address delivery → rt.delivery
       - Wants cart pay → rt.checkout / Pro
