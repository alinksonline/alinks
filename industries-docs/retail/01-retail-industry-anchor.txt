1. WHAT THIS INDUSTRY IS
================================================================================

  Tenants sell PHYSICAL (or packaged) PRODUCTS across any category:
    kitchenware, clothes, fashion, electronics, beauty, toys, hardware,
    packaged groceries, home, sports — “anything they want”.

  They may sell:
    • One brand (own brand / single distributor)
    • Multiple brands (multi-brand store or wholesale market)

  Two commercial modes (diversification spine) — ONBOARDING LABELS FROZEN:
    Retail only           → end consumers (B2C)
    Wholesale only        → bulk / other businesses (B2B)
    Retail + Wholesale    → hybrid (both)

  Catalogue: OPEN — kitchen, clothes, electronics, plumbing, gadgets, mobiles,
  laptops, toys, plants, herbal, Ayurvedic retail, veggies, and anything legal
  they name (doc 14). Capability modules + plan CAPS control cost (doc 15).

  Smart application = one product engine with mode-aware pricing, MOQ, catalog
  presentation, and checkout path — not separate codebases per product type.

================================================================================
2. HOW WE DIVERSIFY (THE MODEL)
================================================================================

  Layer A — MARKET ROLE (who the tenant is)
    retail_shop          neighbourhood / high-street multi-category shop
    multi_brand_store    curated brands, fashion/lifestyle focus
    wholesale_market     bulk market / mandi-style / distributor desk
    brand_dtc            single brand direct-to-consumer
    hybrid_retail_wholesale  both counters (common in India)

  Layer B — TRADE MODE (how each product or catalog sells)
    retail_unit          single unit / consumer pack price
    wholesale_bulk       MOQ + tier prices (10+, 50+, 100+)
    both                 retail AND wholesale prices on same SKU

  Layer C — BRAND GRAPH
    Own brand + third-party brands as catalogue attributes
    Optional “brand pages” on mini-site

  Layer D — FULFILMENT / ORDER PATH
    WhatsApp order (website plan)
    Cart + COD / tenant Razorpay (checkout module)
    Pickup at store / market stall
    Delivery note (not food kitchen board unless they also buy food modules)

  Diversification = Superadmin type + enabled modes + plan limits + UI seeds
  — not infinite separate apps.

================================================================================
3. WHAT ALINKS IS / IS NOT
================================================================================

  IS
    • Multi-brand product catalogue + Store inventory
    • Retail and/or wholesale pricing rules
    • Categories & variations (size, color, pack, unit)
    • Mini-site storefront + WhatsApp + optional cart checkout
    • Leads/enquiries for bulk wholesale
    • Multi-outlet shops (branch pattern 5–10% off)

  IS NOT
    • Artix marketplace taking possession of all sellers’ stock (v1)
    • Payment facilitation of shop GMV by Artix
    • Full WMS / warehouse robotics
    • Restaurant dine-in / kitchen (food_industry)
    • Property sales (real_estate)

================================================================================
4. MODULES (DIRECTION)
================================================================================

  rt.storefront        Public product shop (B2C)
  rt.inventory         Products, stock, SKUs, multi-brand
  rt.categories        Category tree + filters
  rt.variations        Reusable option sets (size/color/pack)
  rt.wholesale         B2B mode: MOQ, tier prices, GSTIN field on order
  rt.multi_brand       Brand entities + brand pages + filters
  rt.checkout          Cart + tenant Razorpay/COD (BYO PG)
  rt.enquiry_bulk      Wholesale enquiry without full cart (optional)
  rt.delivery          Ship to address (online orders — not shop POS)
  rt.pickup            Collect at shop (simple address note OK)

  NOT default: multi-outlet / multi-store POS seats.
  ALINKS is the online mini-site + catalogue, not in-store billing software.
  One brand shop online is enough even if they have several physical shops
  offline (optional plain “pickup address” text later — not a paid multi-store).

  Website plan: presence + limited catalogue + WA order
  Upgrade: full store + wholesale + checkout depth

================================================================================
