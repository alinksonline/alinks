1. TWO PAYMENT WORLDS
================================================================================

  A. PLATFORM BILLING (tenant → Artix)
       Website plans + food module SKUs + combos
       Razorpay (Artix keys) / subscription — gateway must know line items.

  B. TENANT CHECKOUT (customer → shop)
       Food order payment — tenant Razorpay / COD / WhatsApp
       ALINKS does not settle shop sales.

================================================================================
3. PURCHASE SHAPES (À LA CARTE FIRST — PLATFORM-WIDE)
================================================================================

  DEFAULT: À LA CARTE — each module priced and sold SEPARATELY.
  Tenant buys only what they need (see industries-docs/00-MODULE-ALACARTE-PRICING.txt).

  Single module     list monthly/yearly (primary path)
  Combo             OPTIONAL discount pack (10–15% off sum) — never mandatory
  All modules       OPTIONAL type-aware bundle (15–25% off); cloud all excludes dine_in

================================================================================
4. YEARLY CHARM RULE (LOCKED METHOD)
================================================================================

  yearly_raw   = monthly × 12
  yearly_disc  = yearly_raw × (1 − d)     // prefer d=0.10 (10%)
  yearly_price = charm to *99
                 (99, 199, 299, 499, 999, 1999, 2999, 4999, 9999, 19999, …)
  Prefer not exceeding yearly_disc after charm.

  Apply to website plans AND modules AND bundle totals.

================================================================================
5. DISCUSSION PRICE BANDS (₹/MO — NOT FINAL)
================================================================================

  Delivery           299–399
  Pickup             0 with cloud/delivery
  Restaurant Dine-in 499–699
  Catering           399–599
  Kitchen            399–499

  Sweet spot: clear ROI, not agency-priced, not throwaway ₹99 forever.
