06 — MODULE: PROFESSIONAL CONSULTATION (book.professional)
================================================================================
SKU: book.professional
For: coaches, advisors, accountants (non-legal), tutors, therapists (non-clinic
regulated path), general consultants.

================================================================================
1. DEFINITION
================================================================================

  Lightweight professional booking: service types, durations, optional fees,
  calendar. Honor-system compliance (ToS) unless subtype is regulated.

================================================================================
2. WHO CAN BUY
================================================================================

  professional_consult base type.
  Can also attach to general vertical as add-on later.

================================================================================
3. ENABLES
================================================================================

  PUBLIC: service → slot → client details
  ADMIN: consultants as resources, service packages, buffer times
  No medical license gate by default

================================================================================
4. LIFECYCLE (100%)
================================================================================

  Discover → Purchase → Configure services/resources → Operate →
  Integrate payments/reminders → Bill → Disable

================================================================================
5. INVENTORY
================================================================================

  Services: Intro call, Strategy session, Monthly retainers (as packages)
  Categories: by practice area (tenant-defined)
  Variations: Online vs In-person (location modifier), duration

================================================================================
6. INTEGRATION
================================================================================

  + book.core, payments, reminders, multilocation
  + Distinct from book.legal (no legal disclaimers pack) and book.clinic
