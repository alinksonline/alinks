10 — MODULE: REMINDERS & NO-SHOW TOOLS (book.reminders)
================================================================================
SKU: book.reminders

================================================================================
1. DEFINITION
================================================================================

  Automated reminders (SMS/email/WhatsApp where integrated) before appointment
  or event; optional no-show marking and simple policy text.

================================================================================
2. LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

  Purchase → configure channels + timing (24h / 2h) →
  on booking confirm schedule jobs → send → log →
  staff can mark no_show → disable stops new schedules

================================================================================
3. INTEGRATION
================================================================================

  Listens to booking status from book.core + industry packs.
  Uses MSG91/Resend/etc. per integration_setup_docs when wired.
  Venue: remind host 7 days + 1 day before function.
