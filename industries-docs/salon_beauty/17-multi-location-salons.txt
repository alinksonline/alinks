17 — MULTI-LOCATION SALONS (YES — UNLIKE RETAIL WEB-ONLY)
================================================================================

  Salons with 2+ branches need separate staff hours and calendars.
  This IS in scope (physical chairs), unlike retail multi-outlet POS.

  Rules (same pattern as food multi-branch / bookings multi-clinic):
    Primary branch full price
    Extra branches 5–10% off module/seat
    Staff, packages availability, bookings carry branch_id
    Public: pick branch then book

  Not in-store POS billing — still online booking + optional pay-then-book.
