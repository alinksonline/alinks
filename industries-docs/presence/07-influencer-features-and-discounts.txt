1. WHY INFLUENCERS CHOOSE ALINKS PRESENCE
================================================================================

  One mobile-first link they put in Instagram / YouTube / bio:
    • Looks premium (branding, theme, OG share cards)
    • All links + collab path in one place
    • Media kit / rate card without building a full agency site
    • Cheap enough that student / mid-tier creators actually start

  We win on: beauty + simplicity + price for creators + easy upgrade later
  if they open a shop / bookings (other industries).

================================================================================
2. FEATURE PACK — WHAT PRESENCE GIVES (BETTER SET)
================================================================================

  A) ALWAYS ON (Presence core — every Presence tenant)
  ----------------------------------------------------
  pr.presence_core
    • Mini-site, 5-page rule, publish control
    • Branding: logo, colors, fonts, corners, favicon, cover, OG image
    • Profile: photo, display name, tagline, short bio
    • Mobile-first “card” layout (hero → bio → primary CTA → links)

  pr.link_stack   (Link hub — creator core)
    • Unlimited soft-capped link buttons (icons + title + URL)
    • Pin / highlight 1–3 links (e.g. “Latest reel”, “Collab”)
    • Reorder drag-and-drop
    • Social strip: IG, YT, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Maps, custom
    • Optional “opens in new tab” + UTM helper (simple)

  pr.gallery
    • Photo grid (work, events, BTS)
    • Soft caps by plan (higher on Creator Pro)

  pr.contact
    • Call / WhatsApp / Email CTAs
    • Contact form → tenant Google Sheet (optional Connect)
    • Brand collab form fields (brand name, budget range, brief) — still
      only a lead form, NOT paid booking or checkout

  pr.share_kit
    • QR code for the site
    • Share image / OG card (Tap & Blast–style light)
    • Short public URL (handle / custom path)

  YouTube
    • Embed trailers / featured video (YouTube only) OR outbound link
    • Outbound for other platforms (IG, etc.)

  B) CREATOR-STRONG (included on Influencer path / Creator plan)
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  pr.media_kit
    • On-page Media Kit section (not a PDF upload required)
    • Self-reported: niches, platforms, approx reach, past brands
    • Rate card DISPLAY only (packages as text/prices shown — no cart)
    • “Work with me” primary CTA → collab form or WA

  pr.social_proof
    • Testimonials / quotes block
    • “As seen on” / brand logos strip (images + links)
    • Optional follower/count chips (self-entered — not scraped)

  pr.highlights
    • Story-style highlight tiles (e.g. Travel, Food, Tech) → gallery or link

  pr.analytics_lite
    • Page views + link click counts (simple dashboard)
    • Enough to show “my link hub works” — not full marketing suite

  C) PRO / PAID UPGRADES ON PRESENCE (still no sales)
  ---------------------------------------------------
  • Custom domain
  • Remove ALINKS watermark
  • Higher gallery / link / media-kit image caps
  • Priority OG / branded share cards

  D) NEVER ON PRESENCE (LOCKED)
  -----------------------------
  Cart, checkout, COD, Razorpay for goods/services, pay-then-book packages,
  food order, inventory, wholesale, RE sell checkout.
  External “Shop on Instagram” link = OK (off-platform).

5. DISCOUNTS — INFLUENCERS GET GREAT DEALS (LOCKED INTENT)
================================================================================

  Goal: flood of creator signups → brand awareness → later upsell industries.

  A) CREATOR PRICE LADDER (website plan for Presence)
  ---------------------------------------------------
  List prices = normal Website Starter / Pro (same SKUs as other industries).

  Influencer path applies CREATOR DISCOUNT on the website plan only
  (type = influencer_creator, or coupon CREATOR / INFLUENCER):

    Tier                         Discount vs list (recommend)
    ---------------------------  ----------------------------
    Creator Launch (promo)       50–70% off first 12 months
                                 OR free Starter 3 months + 50% year 1 Pro
    Creator ongoing              30–40% off monthly/yearly website plan
    Creator annual               Extra yearly charm …99 + creator % stacked
                                 (cap total discount in Superadmin so margin OK)
    Student / micro-creator      Highest discount or free Starter
                                 (verify optional later: email / self-declare)

  Superadmin fields:
    presence.creator_discount_pct_monthly
    presence.creator_discount_pct_yearly
    presence.creator_launch_coupon (code, % or free months, expiry)
    presence.creator_discount_applies_to = website_plan only
    (modules later if they leave Presence — normal “Select modules” pricing)

  B) WHAT IS FREE / CHEAP FOR CREATORS (product, not only price)
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  Included even on discounted Creator Starter:
    Link hub, profile, contact/collab form, basic gallery, QR, basic share card
  Discounted Creator Pro:
    Media kit + rate card display, social proof, analytics lite, higher caps,
    custom domain at creator price

  C) COUPONS & CAMPAIGNS
  ----------------------
  Suggested codes (examples — configure in Superadmin, not hardcode forever):
    CREATOR50   — 50% off website plan (Presence influencer type)
    LINKHUB     — launch: first year heavy off
    CAMPUS      — student micro-creators
  Seasonal: Diwali / New Year creator flash sales.

  D) REFERRAL (PHASE 2 — design only)
  -----------------------------------
  Creator refers creator → both get free months or extra % off.
  Attracts networks (collab circles).

  E) RULES
  --------
  • Creator discounts do NOT unlock commerce. Still Presence = no sales.
  • Discount is for website plan (and Presence pro add-ons like domain),
    not for unrelated industry modules until they switch industry.
  • **Huge discounts are a trade for promoting ALINKS** — see doc 08
    (Creator Partner tiers, promo duties, fair enforcement).
  • Abuse: one discount per tenant; Superadmin can revoke coupon misuse.
  • Business_profile_only / community types: smaller or no creator discount
    (list price or mild “Presence” promo) — save deep cuts for influencers.

  F) FRONTEND BILLING COPY
  ------------------------
  “Creator pricing” / “Influencer plan”
  “Launch offer — limited time”
  Invoice: Website plan − Creator discount = net
  Never show “à la carte” on screen.
