08 — CREATOR DEAL: DEEP DISCOUNT ↔ PROMOTE ALINKS

WHY
  Huge creator discounts are not free gifts — they are a **trade**:
  ALINKS gives price → creator gives **distribution** (promote ALINKS on their
  channels). Best deals feel fair, simple, and easy to complete — not a
  heavy agency contract.

================================================================================
1. BEST MODEL: “CREATOR PARTNER” (RECOMMENDED)
================================================================================

  Name (frontend):  **Creator Partner**  or  **Creator Promo Plan**
  Not: barter, freebie, influencer marketing (too corporate for micros)

  Structure:  TIERED TRADE  (discount level ↔ promo duty)

  RULE OF THUMB
    Deeper discount  →  clearer, still-light promo obligation
    Mild discount    →  optional soft ask only (share if you love it)

  NEVER make promo so heavy that micro-creators bounce:
    Bad:  “3 Reels/month + Stories every week + 10 tags forever”
    Good: “1 clear shout-out per billing period + ALINKS link in bio/link hub”

================================================================================
2. HOW TO STRIKE THE DEAL (FLOW)
================================================================================

  STEP 1 — Choose Creator path at signup
    Type: influencer_creator
    Screen: “Get Creator pricing — promote ALINKS, keep a pro link hub”

  STEP 2 — Pick discount tier (see §3)
    Show side-by-side: what they pay vs list, what they must post

  STEP 3 — Accept Partner terms (checkbox + timestamp)
    Short, plain language (not 20-page legal first).
    Store acceptance on tenant (DPDP/audit: who agreed when).

  STEP 4 — Activate discount immediately
    Do NOT wait for first post to start the site (friction kills signup).
    Grace window: first promo due within 14–30 days of publish or first
    paid period (Superadmin setting).

  STEP 5 — Easy fulfill
    In dashboard: “Your partner post” checklist + copy kit
    • Suggested caption (EN + optional local)
    • ALINKS referral / handle link
    • 1 image / story frame asset
    • “Mark as posted” + optional URL / screenshot upload

  STEP 6 — Soft compliance
    Reminder at day 10 / day 25 if not marked
    If never fulfilled: warn → next cycle discount reduces or pauses
    Do NOT instantly ban on day 1 — creators need trust

  STEP 7 — Renew each period
    Monthly: 1 light touch OR Story pack
    Yearly:  e.g. 1 Reel/video + 2 Stories + permanent bio link (see tiers)

================================================================================
3. TIER LADDER (BEST BALANCE)
================================================================================

  Tier A — Creator (standard ongoing)     ~30–40% off website plan
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Obligation (light):
    • Keep ALINKS referral or “Built with ALINKS” / partner link on their
      Presence link hub OR Instagram bio (one place is enough)
    • 1 Story OR 1 feed mention per month (or per quarter if micro)
  Soft: optional story template once

  Tier B — Creator Launch (huge)          ~50–70% off first 12 months
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Obligation (clear trade for the deal):
    • Permanent: ALINKS link on Presence link hub (pinned or footer “Made
      with ALINKS — get yours”) for the discounted term
    • Within 30 days of going live: 1 Story + 1 feed post OR 1 short Reel/
      YouTube community post naming ALINKS + their site link
    • Every 90 days: 1 reminder Story (or 1 feed) while discount active
  This is the **main “huge discount” strike deal**.

  Tier C — Creator Pro Partner (deep + Pro features)
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Same as B, plus:
    • Pin “Get your free/cheap site on ALINKS” style link (copy provided)
    • Tag / mention @alinks (or official handle) when platform allows
    • Optional: 1 collab-style Reel quarterly if reach is mid/high

  Tier D — Free / near-free Starter (student / micro launch only)
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Highest obligation relative to price:
    • Bio or link-hub ALINKS link required while free
    • 1 public post in first 14 days
    • Free ends or converts to Tier A if promo never done (after warnings)
  Cap duration (e.g. 3 months free) so it stays acquisition, not freeload.

  Tier E — Mild promo optional            list price or 10–15% off
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  No hard duty. In-app: “Share ALINKS, unlock a thank-you coupon” (opt-in).

  Deep discounts ONLY on influencer_creator + accepted Partner terms.
  business_profile_only does not get Partner deep cuts by default.

================================================================================
4. WHAT “PROMOTE ALINKS” MUST INCLUDE (MINIMUM BRIEF)
================================================================================

  Every required post should carry at least:
    1. Their ALINKS site URL (proof they use the product)
    2. ALINKS name + what it is in one line
       e.g. “My link hub / site is on ALINKS — pro online presence”
    3. Signup / referral link (tracked if possible)
    4. Honest tone — no fake “#ad” spam; if paid-in-kind, FTC/ASCI-style
       disclosure where required: “Partner / discounted with ALINKS”

  Provide ready copy so they do not think:
    “My new page: …  Built with ALINKS. Creators get Creator pricing:
     [referral]. Not a shop — just my links & collabs.”

================================================================================
5. BEST WAY TO MAKE THEM SAY YES (PSYCHOLOGY + UX)
================================================================================

  DO
    • Frame as **partnership**, not “work for free”
    • Show ₹ saved in big numbers next to 1–2 simple duties
    • Activate site first; promo after (reduce fear)
    • One checklist, one due date, assets ready
    • Let them choose channel they already use (IG Story vs YT Community)
    • Yearly plan = fewer promo events (creators hate monthly content jobs)
    • Referral bonus on top (they earn if friends sign up) — feels win-win

  DON’T
    • Demand exclusive content rights or usage of their face forever without
      separate consent
    • Require paid ads spend
    • Force commerce modules
    • Hide the promo duty after payment (show before accept)
    • Shame publicly if they miss a post — private dashboard only

  STRIKE LANGUAGE (onboarding)
    “Creator Partner: up to 70% off your first year.
     You keep a pro link hub. You mention ALINKS a few times.
     One checkbox — that’s the deal.”

================================================================================
6. ENFORCEMENT (FAIR, NOT TOXIC)
================================================================================

  Status on tenant:
    partner_tier
    partner_terms_accepted_at
    partner_promo_due_by
    partner_promo_last_fulfilled_at
    partner_compliance: good | warning | reduced | paused

  Policy:
    Miss 1 cycle  →  reminder + 7-day grace
    Miss 2        →  discount steps down one tier next bill
    Miss ongoing  →  back to list price; site stays (no hostage delete)
    Fraud (fake screenshot, never published) → revoke Partner discount

  Superadmin can whitelist VIPs (manual partners, higher reach) with custom
  duties written in notes.

================================================================================
7. TRACKING (KEEP SIMPLE)
================================================================================

  Phase 1 (notes / MVP intent):
    Self-report URL + optional screenshot
    Manual spot-check Superadmin

  Phase 2:
    Unique referral codes per creator
    Count signups attributed → bonus free months

  Do not promise automated IG API verification on day one.

================================================================================
8. LEGAL / TRUST (LIGHT)
================================================================================

  Partner terms one-pager:
    • Discount is conditional on promo duties while Partner active
    • Creator owns their content; ALINKS may reshare with credit if they
      opt-in checkbox “ALINKS may repost my mention”
    • Disclosure if jurisdiction requires
    • Cancel Partner anytime → discount ends next cycle; data export OK
    • Not employment; not guaranteed follower growth

  Lawyer review before production terms page (pointer only in notes).

================================================================================
9. WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================

  • Pay cash to every micro for the same discount (kills margin) — use
    product discount + promo trade first
  • Only discount with zero ask (you pay twice: margin + no awareness)
  • Only heavy contract with big influencers and ignore micros — micros
    are volume for ALINKS brand
  • Bundle discount with forced retail/food modules

================================================================================
10. RECOMMENDED DEFAULT LOCK
================================================================================

  Primary strike deal for “huge discount”:
    **Tier B — Creator Launch**
    50–70% year 1
    ↔  link hub/bio ALINKS link for term
    ↔  1 launch post pack within 30 days
    ↔  1 light reminder every 90 days

  Ongoing after year 1:
    **Tier A** 30–40% ↔ bio/link-hub mention + light monthly/quarterly

  Free micro:
    **Tier D** short free window ↔ link + first post, then A or list

  Frontend labels:
    “Creator Partner” / “Creator pricing” / “Partner duties”
    Never “à la carte”; never bury the trade.
