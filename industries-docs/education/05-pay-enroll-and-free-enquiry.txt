================================================================================
05 — FREE ENQUIRY / DEMO VS PAID ENROLL
================================================================================

================================================================================
1. FREE ALWAYS ALLOWED
================================================================================

  • Course enquiry form / WhatsApp
  • Free demo slot (online or offline)
  • Free tuition slots if teacher chooses
  No Razorpay required to operate education site + calendar.

================================================================================
2. PAID OPTIONAL (TENANT PG)
================================================================================

  • Course seat deposit / full fee
  • Paid demo token
  • Paid 1:1 session (pay_then_book like salon)
  Money → teacher/institute Razorpay — not Artix settlement

================================================================================
3. SCHOOLS & COLLEGES
================================================================================

  Usually free admissions enquiry + optional campus visit slot.
  Fee collection online is optional module; many stay enquiry-only.

================================================================================
4. INDEPENDENT TEACHERS
================================================================================

  Side income: start free demos + YouTube → charge later for slots.
