================================================================================
07 — INDEPENDENT TEACHERS / SIDE INCOME
================================================================================

================================================================================
1. UNDERSTANDING
================================================================================

  School/college teachers, freelancers, musicians, skill coaches who want
  EXTRA INCOME with their OWN brand mini-site — not buried under an institute.

  Onboarding type: independent_teacher | online_tutor_creator

================================================================================
2. WHAT THEY GET
================================================================================

  • Own handle (e.g. alinks.online/priya-maths or custom domain later)
  • Own courses / subjects list (any subject — maths, guitar, English, …)
  • YouTube embeds (intro videos, free tutorials funnel)
  • Live online or offline tuition SLOTS
  • Free booking by default; optional paid sessions (their Razorpay)
  • Google Calendar FREE so personal teaching calendar stays in sync
  • WhatsApp CTA for quick enquiries
  • Leads inbox (parent/student enquiries)

================================================================================
3. WHAT THEY DO NOT NEED
================================================================================

  • Multi-faculty complexity (optional hide)
  • Multi-center (unless they travel — single “home/online” location)
  • School ERP

================================================================================
4. PRICING FRIENDLY
================================================================================

  Lower caps / Teacher Starter plan so side-hustle cost stays low.
  Still product caps (anti-loss) but affordable entry.

================================================================================
5. INSTITUTE VS INDEPENDENT
================================================================================

  Institute: many faculty under one brand
  Independent: one person brand; may later “join” an institute as faculty
  on their site only if we add that link later — NOT required v1
