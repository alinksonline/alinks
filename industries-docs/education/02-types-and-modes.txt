================================================================================
1. TENANT TYPES (SUPERADMIN / ONBOARDING)
================================================================================

  SLUG                    Label                         Typical user
  ----------------------  ----------------------------  ----------------------
  school                  School                        School marketing site
  college                 College / university brand    Admissions marketing
  coaching_institute      Coaching institute            JEE/NEET/competitive
  tuition_center          Tuition classes               Subject tuitions batch
  music_arts_school       Music / arts school           Music, dance, art (seed)
  skill_academy           Skill development             Vocational / pro skills
  independent_teacher     Independent teacher/tutor     Side income / solo brand
  online_tutor_creator    Online tutor / creator        YouTube + courses + slots
  hybrid_institute        Institute + online            Offline center + live

  Music is a TYPE/seed, not the only skill vertical — skill_academy and
  independent_teacher can teach ANY subject (coding, guitar, maths, painting…).

================================================================================
2. DELIVERY MODES (PER COURSE / SLOT)
================================================================================

  offline     At center / home tuition address
  online      Live slot with Meet/Zoom/YouTube Live / custom link
  hybrid      Student chooses or course offers both
  async_link  Recorded tutorial — YouTube video/playlist only (no live slot)

================================================================================
3. COMMERCIAL MODES (STUDENT-FACING)
================================================================================

  free_enquiry       Form / WA only
  free_demo_slot     Book demo without pay
  free_tuition_slot  Ongoing free slots (rare; allowed)
  pay_deposit        Token then confirm (tenant PG)
  pay_full           Full fee online (tenant PG)
  pay_at_center      Book free; pay offline

  Default: free enquiry + free demo available without Razorpay.

================================================================================
