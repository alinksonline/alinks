================================================================================
01 — AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY ANCHOR
================================================================================

================================================================================
1. WHAT IT IS
================================================================================

  Automotive tenants run mini-sites for:
    • New/used vehicle listings (enquiry, not platform title sale)
    • Workshop service booking (wash, service, detailing packages)
    • Spare parts / accessories shop (retail)
    • Driving school demos (slots)

  Diversification by TYPE + which modules they enable — one industry pack.

================================================================================
2. TYPES
================================================================================

  car_dealer_new, used_car_dealer, two_wheeler_dealer,
  multi_brand_auto_dealer, service_workshop, car_detailing,
  spare_parts_shop, driving_school, fleet_b2b

================================================================================
3. MODULES
================================================================================

  auto.vehicle_listings   Inventory of vehicles (sell interest / display)
  auto.leads              Test drive / buy enquiry CRM-lite
  auto.service_packages   Service & detailing packages
  auto.service_booking    Slot booking for workshop
  auto.parts_retail       Parts catalogue (retail engine)
  auto.wholesale_parts    Optional B2B parts (retail wholesale pattern)
  auto.delivery           Parts delivery (retail delivery pattern)
  auto.multilocation      Multi showroom/workshop (5–10% off extra) YES

  google.calendar FREE for service appointments

================================================================================
4. NO PLATFORM VEHICLE SALES
================================================================================

  Like real estate: high-ticket vehicle purchase is offline (loan, RC, insurance).
  ALINKS: photos, specs, “Price on request” / indicative, WhatsApp dealer.
  Optional: “Book test drive” slot free.

================================================================================
5. VS OTHER PACKS
================================================================================

  Vehicle listing privacy: optional teaser mode if used-car brokers fear
  poaching (reuse RE Property-Bank open/teaser/private idea lightly).
  Service booking ≈ salon packages + staff (mechanics).
  Parts ≈ retail multi-brand.
