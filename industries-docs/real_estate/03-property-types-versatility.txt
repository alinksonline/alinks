================================================================================
03 — PROPERTY / OFFERING TYPES (VERSATILITY)
================================================================================

Superadmin subtypes under Real Estate industry group. Each shapes fields & seeds.

================================================================================
1. BUILDERS
================================================================================

  Focus: Project → Towers/Phases → Unit types (1/2/3 BHK)
  Fields: RERA id (optional display), possession, status, amenities, gallery
  CTA: Enquire, Download brochure (link/file), Schedule visit
  Not: unit e-commerce checkout

================================================================================
2. CONSTRUCTION COMPANIES
================================================================================

  Focus: Portfolio of works, services (turnkey, contracting), B2B enquiry
  Listings may be “completed projects” case studies more than sellable units
  CTA: Get quote / Contact

================================================================================
3. LAND / PLOT SALES
================================================================================

  Focus: Plot inventory — area, facing, location, survey ref (display)
  Price: “On request” or indicative (display only)
  CTA: WhatsApp plot code, call, visit land

================================================================================
4. VENTURES
================================================================================

  Focus: Opportunity narrative, partners, investment enquiry (not equity KYC)
  CTA: Partner / Investor enquiry form

================================================================================
5. GATED COMMUNITIES
================================================================================

  Focus: Community brand + internal plots/villas phases
  Amenities storytelling high
  CTA: Community visit + plot interest

================================================================================
6. VILLAS / VILLA PROJECTS
================================================================================

  Focus: Villa product cards (type, area, bedrooms) as listings
  Still enquiry-only “sales”

================================================================================
7. SHARED LISTING KINDS (DATA)
================================================================================

  project | phase | unit | plot | land_parcel | villa | portfolio_case
  All map to re.listings inventory (doc 04) with type-specific field sets.
