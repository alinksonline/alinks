1. WHAT ALINKS IS (AND IS NOT) FOR REAL ESTATE
================================================================================

  ALINKS IS
    • Mobile-first mini-sites for builders, construction brands, land sellers,
      ventures, gated communities, villa projects
    • Property / project / plot LISTINGS (catalogue & storytelling)
    • Agent network: one agent, many companies/projects to promote
    • Direct company sales desk: staff sell their own inventory
    • Lead capture: WhatsApp, call, enquiry form, “Schedule site visit”
    • CRM-lite for leads (status: new → contacted → visit → negotiation → won/lost)
    • Optional site-visit booking (calendar slots — free client booking mode)

  ALINKS IS NOT
    • Online property marketplace checkout (no pay-for-deed on platform)
    • Platform sales facilitation / escrow of land money
    • RERA registration portal or title registry
    • Full ERP for construction accounting

  WHY NO ONLINE SALES
    Property purchase is high-ticket, regulated, offline paper + bank.
    Product trust = enquiry quality, not fake “Buy ₹48L now” cart.
    Same philosophy as “no Artix settlement of shop GMV” — here even stronger.

================================================================================
2. VERSATILITY — WHO USES IT
================================================================================

  A. BUILDERS
     Multi-tower / multi-phase projects, brochures, unit types, status
     (under construction / ready), floor plans, amenity storytelling.

  B. CONSTRUCTION COMPANIES
     Portfolio of completed + ongoing works, B2B enquiry, tender interest,
     less “plot inventory”, more capability + project gallery.

  C. LAND / PLOT SALES
     Survey numbers, area (sq yd / acre), location pins, price on request
     or indicative price (display only — not cart).

  D. VENTURES / JOINT DEVELOPMENT
     Project brand, partners, opportunity overview, investor/partner enquiry.

  E. GATED COMMUNITIES
     Community brand, plots/villas inside, amenities, phase-wise availability.

  F. VILLAS / VILLA PROJECTS
     Individual villa SKUs as listings (type, bhk, area), still enquiry-only.

  Same “Real Estate” industry family; Superadmin subtypes + modules shape UI.

================================================================================
3. TWO SELLER PATHS (CORE DESIGN)
================================================================================

  PATH 1 — COMPANY (SEPARATE SITE)
    Tenant = Builder / Construction / Land / Venture / Gated / Villa brand.
    Property-Bank = their listings only (sell/resale/rent/lease).
    Staff = company sales team.
    Leads = company only.
    Website = company brand only.

  PATH 2 — AGENT (SEPARATE SITE — NOT MIXED WITH COMPANY ACCOUNT)
    Tenant = Agent / Agency brand — fully separate signup, billing, handle.
    Property-Bank = agent’s own portfolio (may market many builders’ projects
    as their listings; same real-world project may appear on many agent sites).
    No company-approval link required in v1 (doc 15).
    Leads = agent only.
    Website = agent brand only.

  PROPERTY-BANK PRIVACY (doc 14)
    UI name: Property-Bank.
    Modes: OPEN | TEASER (default for protective agents) | PRIVATE.
    Full detail not exposed to other agents; price display = owner’s wish.

  DEAL TYPES (doc 16) — both company and agent:
    Sell | Resale | Rent | Lease

  PLAN LIMITS (doc 11)
    e.g. 10 / 50 / 200 projects or listings by plan tier.

4. MODULES (DISCUSSION → LOCKED DIRECTION)
================================================================================

  re.listings          Property-Bank catalogue (core RE; sell/resale/rent/lease)
  re.leads             Enquiry CRM-lite
  re.agent_desk        Agent tenant workspace + agent mini-site features
  re.project_showcase  Rich builder/gated/villa storytelling (company sites)
  re.site_visit        Visit booking (slots; free for visitors by default)
  re.staff_sales       Company sales team roles
  re.multilocation     Multiple sites/offices (5–10% off extra — same pattern)

  Website plan (Layer 1): brand site + featured projects + WA/call CTAs.
  No “property cart / checkout” module — ever as default RE commerce.

================================================================================
5. RELATION TO OTHER INDUSTRIES
================================================================================

  book.core / site_visit
    Optional reuse of bookings engine for “Schedule visit” only.
  food / banquet
    Event halls are bookings_industry venue — not RE land sales.
  Google Calendar (FREE)
    Site visits, agent meetings → tenant Gmail calendar.

================================================================================
6. BILLING LAYERS (SAME PHILOSOPHY AS FOOD/BOOKINGS)
================================================================================

  LAYER 1 — Website plan (marketing site): presence + listings display + WA
  LAYER 2 — RE modules (agent desk, CRM depth, multi-project limits)
  NEVER — take % of property sale on platform as default product

================================================================================
7. SUCCESS METRICS (PRODUCT)
================================================================================

  • Lead volume & quality (contact rate)
  • Time-to-first listing for builder
  • Agent can attach 2+ companies without confusion
  • Zero accidental “buy property online” UX
