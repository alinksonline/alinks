1. PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

  Every listing carries a deal_type. Agents and companies can both offer any
  mix of Sell / Resale / Rent / Lease (subject to plan limits and law).

  Still NO platform online completion of sale/rent payment for the property
  asset itself — enquiry / visit / offline paperwork.

================================================================================
2. DEFINITIONS (PRODUCT + UI COPY)
================================================================================

  SELL (New / Primary)
    First sale from developer/builder/land owner path as “new inventory”.
    Typical: new launch flats, builder plots, villa project primary.
    UI label: “For sale” + badge “New” optional.
    Who: builders, companies, agents marketing primary stock.

  RESALE
    Secondary market — owner already holds title; selling again.
    Typical: resale flat, resale plot, resale villa in gated community.
    UI label: “Resale”.
    Extra fields often: age of property, floor, furnishing, owner type
    (display only; no owner phone on teaser).
    Who: agents heavily; companies sometimes for buy-back inventory.

  RENT
    Short-to-medium residential/commercial let; recurring occupation.
    UI label: “For rent”.
    Fields: monthly rent (display mode per wish), deposit note (display),
    available_from, furnishing, preferred tenant notes (optional).
    Who: agents + companies (builder rental stock, company staff housing, etc.).

  LEASE
    Longer-term contractual occupation (often commercial / industrial /
    farm land / institutional). Distinct from month-to-month “rent” in UI
    even if law overlaps — product treats as separate deal_type for filters.
    UI label: “For lease”.
    Fields: lease_term_years_or_months, lock_in note, rent_escalation note
    (all display/enquiry — not e-sign lease v1).
    Who: land companies, commercial agents, ventures.

  SUMMARY TABLE
    deal_type | Intent                         | Typical duration signal
    --------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------
    sell      | Transfer ownership (primary)   | One-time sale
    resale    | Transfer ownership (secondary) | One-time sale
    rent      | Temporary occupation           | Monthly-oriented
    lease     | Longer contractual occupation  | Multi-month / multi-year

================================================================================
3. FILTERS & PUBLIC UX
================================================================================

  Mini-site nav / chips:
    All | Sale | Resale | Rent | Lease
  (Sale may group sell+resale with subfilter, or keep separate — prefer separate
   chips so resale seekers are not lost.)

  Card badges: NEW SALE | RESALE | RENT | LEASE

  Search: city + deal_type + property_kind (plot/flat/villa/land/farm/…)

================================================================================
4. PRICE DISPLAY (OWNER’S WISH)
================================================================================

  LOCKED: Company or Agent who owns the listing chooses:

    price_display_mode:
      hidden           — no number
      on_request       — “Price on request”
      indicative       — show number as indicative (not checkout)
      price_band       — e.g. “₹40–50L” / “₹25k–30k / mo”

  No platform force of “always on request” or “always show price”.

================================================================================
5. PROPERTY-BANK + DEAL TYPE
================================================================================

  Each Property-Bank item has deal_type + visibility_mode (open/teaser/private).
  Teaser still hides sensitive fields regardless of sell/rent/lease.

  Example:
    Resale flat + teaser → public sees “Resale 2BHK · Whitefield · On request”
    Full address only after lead to that agent/company.
